# Hey, I'm Jeff 👋

I started my career coaching track and field at the collegiate level, including roles at Troy University and Piedmont College.

Coaching taught me to break difficult performance into teachable steps, identify what is blocking progress, build repeatable systems, measure results, and eventually transfer capability to the person doing the work.

Years later, I realized those same principles are incredibly useful in AI adoption.

I moved into managed technology in 2019 and now lead voice, data, systems, and operational work for a technology company supporting more than 1,000 customer locations. My background spans business operations, customer-facing technology, workflow design, networking, Microsoft 365, automation, employee development, and AI-assisted solution development.

Over time, I found myself increasingly drawn to one kind of problem:

> How do we take work that is manual, fragmented, or dependent on one person's expertise and turn it into a system other people can execute confidently?

That's what I'm focused on now.

## How I Work

My approach to technology transformation is generally:

**Discover → Define → Prioritize → Design → Pilot → Deploy → Measure**

For AI adoption specifically:

**Understand → Enable → Support → Measure → Improve → Scale**

I tend to work at the intersection of three disciplines:

**Operator**
I understand customers, service delivery, budgets, vendors, commercial decisions, employee constraints, and what happens when a system fails in the real world.

**Integrator**
I connect business requirements with workflows, software, APIs, automation, AI, and technical infrastructure.

**Coach**
I care about whether the person using the system actually becomes capable without depending permanently on the person who built it.
